Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Servmor ("we," "us," or "our") operates Syncmor, an SMS-based employee scheduling service, and uses text messaging (SMS) to communicate with our employees about work scheduling, including requesting availability and sending finalized shift schedules.

Information we collect

We collect employee names and mobile phone numbers, provided directly by the employee to their manager as part of the hiring/onboarding process. We also store the content of scheduling-related text messages sent and received.

How we use this information

We use this information solely to operate our internal employee scheduling system: sending availability requests, receiving availability replies, and sending finalized work schedules.

No sharing of mobile numbers

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Mobile information is used only for the scheduling purposes described in this policy and is never shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional use.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on scheduling needs, typically 2–4 messages per employee per week (availability requests and published schedules).

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages. Reply HELP for help. You may also contact your manager directly to be removed from text-based scheduling.

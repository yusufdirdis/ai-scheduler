Servmor operates Syncmor, an SMS-based employee scheduling program ("the Program"). By providing your mobile number to your manager and agreeing to participate, you consent to receive text messages from Servmor related to work scheduling.

Program description

The Program sends employees: (1) periodic requests for their work availability, and (2) finalized work schedules with a link to view shift details. Employees may reply to availability requests with their available days and times.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on scheduling needs, typically 2–4 messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from the Program. Reply HELP for assistance. You can also opt out by notifying your manager directly.
